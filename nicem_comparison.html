01

Original review — six points, resolved

An expert reviewer raised six methodological concerns on an earlier version of this analysis, before NICEM's independent replication existed. All six were resolved at the time; they are reproduced here in full because the new NICEM evidence (§03) independently reinforces two of them in a way the reviewer could not have seen originally.

1Reported heterozygosity (0.0036%) is inconsistent with an apparent two-peak k-mer spectrum; the second peak may reflect duplication or repeat content rather than true heterozygosity. Recommended Smudgeplot and self-synteny analysis.

Response

Confirmed founded, and now directly measured. Self-synteny blocks at 100.0% identity, elevated pre-purge BUSCO duplication (14.9%/10.1%, collapsing to 0.5%/1.2% post-correction), and purge_dups flagging 23%/19% of each raw assembly as duplicate content together explained the original inconsistency. Smudgeplot — the direct, purpose-built test — independently confirms a genuine AB heterozygosity signal in both strains with no secondary AAB duplication peak: heterozygosity and duplication are two real, coexisting properties of this genome, not one masking the other.

Status: resolved
2HS2 (66.6 Mb) and ALE (64.5 Mb) assembly sizes differ by ~2.1 Mb — real deletion, or an assembly-processing artifact?

Response

Traced to root cause: purge_dups had been applied to HS2 only, with miscalibrated automatic cutoffs. Rerun consistently on both assemblies with coverage-anchored manual cutoffs. Once purged identically, the gap shrinks from 2.1 Mb to 0.87 Mb and reverses direction — the original difference was predominantly a processing artifact, not a real ALE-lineage deletion. Current corrected sizes: HS2 51.2 Mb, ALE 52.1 Mb.

Status: resolved
3T2T completion isn't required for this comparison, but caution is warranted for SNPs/SVs near contig ends.

Response

Agreed. Per-contig telomere classification is propagated as a confidence annotation on every reported variant. This figure was found stale during a later audit and recomputed against the corrected assembly and current 30-candidate funnel. Of 78 current HS2 contigs, none have confirmed telomere signal at both ends (24 single-end, 54 neither) — stated plainly, as expected for a draft, non-T2T assembly. Of the 30 sure candidates, 4 (all unnamed, intergenic) sit within 20kb of an unconfirmed contig end; 0 of the 9 named candidates discussed anywhere in this project are affected.

Status: resolved — corrected: 4/30 sure candidates flagged (0/9 named)
4If ALE is a direct HS2-derived mutant, structural differences should be minimal — given the duplication signal, the synteny figure should show duplicated regions as boxes, which it currently does not.

Response

Correctly identified a gap between two analyses run separately but never combined: the existing figure showed only cross-genome synteny restricted to uniquely-matching contigs, which by construction excludes duplicated/multi-mapping content.

Status: resolved — figure rebuilt combining cross-genome synteny (184 blocks) with within-genome duplicated-block coordinates (10,248 HS2 / 10,291 ALE)
5In a duplicated genome, "1:1" orthology/synteny assignments risk misassigning paralogs.

Response

Agreed — a material risk given confirmed duplication content. Every candidate is cross-referenced against self-synteny duplicated-block coordinates, extended into an explicit, unconditional pass/fail criterion applied genome-wide, not a spot-check on pre-selected genes.

Status: resolved — "clear of duplication" is 1 of 5 criteria applied to all ~4,146 genome-wide candidate sites
6Requested the k-mer analysis, Smudgeplot, self-synteny analysis, and exact workflow/options for closer review.

Response

The complete pipeline is documented command-by-command in the full technical report, including every non-default parameter and the reasoning behind it.

Status: resolved
02

Methods comparison: our pipeline vs. NICEM's

NICEM received the same raw PacBio HiFi reads and worked entirely independently, with no access to our assembly, our annotation, or our candidate list. One naming note for the record: NICEM's own files and reports label the HS2 ancestor strain simply as “HS” — a shorthand on their end, not a different strain.

Assembly

Ours: hifiasm → purge_dups with coverage-anchored manual cutoffs, applied identically to both strains → polishing.

NICEM: hifiasm, no haplotig purging applied to either strain.

Annotation

Ours: AUGUSTUS ab initio (species model, 9,388 genes) cross-checked against GMAP/Trinity transcript evidence.

NICEM: AUGUSTUS ab initio on their own assembly (11,005 genes) with Blast2GO functional annotation.

Variant calling

Ours: bcftools + GATK, cross-validated in both reference directions, plus pbsv/Sniffles2 for structural variants.

NICEM comparison: minimap2 (asm5) + paftools.js call, restricted to uniquely-alignable regions — the correct choice for a heavily duplicated genome, but it means only 22.6% of the genome (26.3 Mb) is testable at all.

Candidate validation

Ours: explicit 5-criterion funnel (GATK agreement, reciprocal-mapping consistency, short-read support, clear-of-duplication, repeat-artifact screening) applied genome-wide, yielding 30 sure candidates.

NICEM: raw paftools.js output, no equivalent multi-criterion funnel applied.

03

Assembly quality: independently converging on the same picture

The key connection to the original review

Review point 1 asked whether an apparent second k-mer peak reflected duplication rather than heterozygosity. NICEM's raw, unpurged assembly — built with zero knowledge of our purge_dups results — shows exactly that pattern independently: massive apparent size inflation from unpurged duplicate content, which collapses once purging is applied. A second, fully independent team hit the same wall our own earlier draft hit, for the same underlying reason.

Candidate-level cross-validation

NICEM's independent paftools.js variant calls were then checked at each lifted-over position.

05

Why missense counts differ between the two analyses

Our own funnel-validated "sure candidates" (n=30) were individually classified for coding consequence against the AUGUSTUS gene model: 20 intronic, 5 intergenic, 1 indel, and 4 real missense substitutions (MASY Trp38Arg; three previously-unnamed genes: Leu82Pro, Ala350Asp, Phe207Ser). NICEM's own genome-wide single-copy substitution set, classified the same way, produced only one coding-consequence hit pair (a nonsense/missense call at two adjacent bases in the same gene) — the same adjacent-position clustering pattern already identified as a local-alignment artifact signature elsewhere in NICEM's data, not a credible independent finding.

This is an absence, not a disagreement

Checked directly: none of our 4 real missense positions have any variant call in NICEM's dataset at all. All four fall inside the ~77% of the genome NICEM's unpurged, duplication-heavy assembly cannot uniquely align. NICEM's pipeline never tested these positions — it did not test them and find something different.

Restricting to single-copy regions should improve signal quality if the remaining variants were real; instead Ts/Tv dropped further below the random-chance floor (0.5), and the overwhelming majority of indels sit in homopolymer tracts — a textbook alignment artifact, not biology. By contrast, our own ALE-specific substitution set is enriched to 44% for the C>A/G>T oxidative-damage signature (chance level: 33% for 2 of 6 transversion classes) — a real, non-random mutational fingerprint. No additional credible candidates survive NICEM's genome-wide data.

07

Summary verdict & what remains open

What this establishes
  • All six original review points are resolved, and two (heterozygosity/duplication, assembly-size gap) are now independently reinforced by a second team's assembly hitting the same underlying issue.
  • 3 of 9 point candidates and 1 of 2 structural variants are confirmed by a fully independent assembly and variant-calling pipeline, at exact coordinates.
  • A systematic genome-wide re-screen of NICEM's own data found no additional credible candidates — the signal-quality tests (Ts/Tv, homopolymer fraction) point the same direction our own artifact-filtering already did.
Honestly still open
  • 6 of 9 point candidates and 1 structural variant remain untestable by NICEM specifically, due to their assembly's limited unique-alignment fraction — not contradicted, but not independently confirmed either.
  • The three NICEM-confirmed point mutations (EPHA, CLPB3, SCP46) are intronic relative to the current gene models, not missense — the causal mechanism for these three is not yet established at the protein-coding level.
  • Wet-lab Sanger confirmation of the named candidates has not yet been performed; primers are designed but unconfirmed.
