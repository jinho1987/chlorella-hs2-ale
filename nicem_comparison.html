Original review — six points, resolved

An expert reviewer raised six methodological concerns on an earlier version of this analysis, before NICEM's independent replication existed. All six were resolved at the time; they are reproduced here in full because the new NICEM evidence (§03) independently reinforces two of them in a way the reviewer could not have seen originally.

1Reported heterozygosity (0.0036%) is inconsistent with an apparent two-peak k-mer spectrum; the second peak may reflect duplication or repeat content rather than true heterozygosity. Recommended Smudgeplot and self-synteny analysis.

Response

Confirmed founded, and now directly measured — and updated 2026-09-04 with the properly-run algorithm (the original plotting frontend was silently failing on an argument bug; see the correction on the main site, §08). Self-synteny blocks at 100.0% identity, elevated pre-purge BUSCO duplication (14.9%/10.1%, collapsing to 0.5%/1.2% post-correction), and purge_dups flagging 23%/19% of each raw assembly as duplicate content together explained the original inconsistency. Smudgeplot — the direct, purpose-built test, now run correctly — confirms AB (heterozygous-diploid) as the dominant single k-mer-pair class in both strains (44% HS2, 63% ALE), with real secondary AAB/AAAB/AABB content (26–56% combined) that reflects genome-wide repeat/duplication rather than a competing ploidy signal: heterozygosity and duplication are two real, coexisting, independently-quantified properties of this genome, not one masking the other.

Status: resolved
2HS2 (66.6 Mb) and ALE (64.5 Mb) assembly sizes differ by ~2.1 Mb — real deletion, or an assembly-processing artifact?

Response

Traced to root cause: purge_dups had been applied to HS2 only, with miscalibrated automatic cutoffs. Rerun consistently on both assemblies with coverage-anchored manual cutoffs. Once purged identically, the gap shrinks from 2.1 Mb to 0.87 Mb and reverses direction — the original difference was predominantly a processing artifact, not a real ALE-lineage deletion. Current corrected sizes: HS2 51.2 Mb, ALE 52.1 Mb.

Status: resolved
3T2T completion isn't required for this comparison, but caution is warranted for SNPs/SVs near contig ends.

Response

Agreed. Per-contig telomere classification is propagated as a confidence annotation on every reported variant. This figure was found stale during a later audit and recomputed against the corrected assembly and the 30-candidate funnel current at that time. Of 78 current HS2 contigs, none have confirmed telomere signal at both ends (24 single-end, 54 neither) — stated plainly, as expected for a draft, non-T2T assembly. Of the 30 sure candidates then current, 4 (all unnamed, intergenic) sit within 20kb of an unconfirmed contig end; 0 of the 9 named candidates discussed anywhere in this project are affected. Not yet re-verified against the later 24-candidate set (2026-09-02) — worth checking whether any of the 4 flagged positions are among the 6 since-retracted false positives before citing this figure again.

Status: resolved — as measured against the 30-candidate set; re-verification against the current 24 is an open item
4If ALE is a direct HS2-derived mutant, structural differences should be minimal — given the duplication signal, the synteny figure should show duplicated regions as boxes, which it currently does not.

Response

Correctly identified a gap between two analyses run separately but never combined: the existing figure showed only cross-genome synteny restricted to uniquely-matching contigs, which by construction excludes duplicated/multi-mapping content.

Status: resolved — figure rebuilt combining cross-genome synteny (184 blocks) with within-genome duplicated-block coordinates (10,248 HS2 / 10,291 ALE)
5In a duplicated genome, "1:1" orthology/synteny assignments risk misassigning paralogs.

Response

Agreed — a material risk given confirmed duplication content. Every candidate is cross-referenced against self-synteny duplicated-block coordinates, extended into an explicit, unconditional pass/fail criterion applied genome-wide, not a spot-check on pre-selected genes.

Status: resolved — "clear of duplication" is 1 of 5 criteria applied to all ~4,146 genome-wide candidate sites
6Requested the k-mer analysis, Smudgeplot, self-synteny analysis, and exact workflow/options for closer review.

Response

k-mer/heterozygosity: FastK (k=21) → hetmers → peak_agregation. Coverage anchors cross-checked two independent ways: hifiasm's internal ha_pt_gen/purge_dups pass (homozygous coverage threshold HS2 = 157–158×, ALE = 204×) and the raw total-bases÷coverage k-mer estimate — the two agree, and that agreement anchors every downstream coverage-dependent parameter.
Smudgeplot — correction, 2026-09-04: hetmers was originally crashing silently on an argument bug (an internal -e value passed as the literal string "None" instead of an integer), producing no k-mer-pair output at all; this was worked around at the time by hand-classifying the raw coverage data directly rather than running the tool's own pair-detection algorithm, and that workaround's output was reported here as "no secondary AAB peak." That characterization has since been corrected: the underlying bug was fixed (the tool's own cutoff subcommand gives the required integer error threshold — L=30 HS2, L=25 ALE) and the actual algorithm was run end-to-end. The real result: AB (heterozygous-diploid) is the dominant single k-mer-pair class in both strains (44% HS2, 63% ALE), with real secondary AAB/AAAB/AABB content (26–56% combined) reflecting genome-wide repeat/duplication — independently consistent with the BUSCO pre/post-purge duplication numbers above, not a contradiction of the diploidy call. Full breakdown and figures: main site §08.
Self-synteny: minimap2 -DP (self-alignment, duplicate/paralog mode) → bedtools merge on the resulting block coordinates → near-100.0%-identity blocks flagged as duplicate content, cross-referenced against every candidate as an unconditional pass/fail funnel criterion.
Full command-by-command detail, every tool version, and every non-default flag — from raw reads through the final candidate list — is in the pipeline/methods section of the complete technical report: report8.html § Methods →

02

Methods comparison: our pipeline vs. NICEM's

NICEM received the same raw PacBio HiFi reads and worked entirely independently, with no access to our assembly, our annotation, or our candidate list. One naming note for the record: NICEM's own files and reports label the HS2 ancestor strain simply as “HS” — a shorthand on their end, not a different strain.

Assembly

Ours: hifiasm → purge_dups with coverage-anchored manual cutoffs, applied identically to both strains → polishing.

NICEM: hifiasm, no haplotig purging applied to either strain.

Annotation

Ours: AUGUSTUS ab initio (species model, 9,388 genes) cross-checked against GMAP/Trinity transcript evidence.

NICEM: AUGUSTUS ab initio on their own assembly (11,005 genes) with Blast2GO functional annotation.

Variant calling

Ours: bcftools + GATK, cross-validated in both reference directions, plus pbsv/Sniffles2 for structural variants.

NICEM comparison: minimap2 (asm5) + paftools.js call, restricted to uniquely-alignable regions — the correct choice for a heavily duplicated genome, but it means only 22.6% of the genome (26.3 Mb) is testable at all.

Candidate validation

Ours: explicit 5-criterion funnel (GATK agreement, reciprocal-mapping consistency, short-read support, clear-of-duplication, repeat-artifact screening) applied genome-wide, yielding 24 sure candidates (originally 30; 6 retracted as false positives by a later funnel-gap audit — see the correction note above).

NICEM: raw paftools.js output, no equivalent multi-criterion funnel applied.

03

Assembly quality: independently converging on the same picture

The key connection to the original review

Review point 1 asked whether an apparent second k-mer peak reflected duplication rather than heterozygosity. NICEM's raw, unpurged assembly — built with zero knowledge of our purge_dups results — shows exactly that pattern independently: massive apparent size inflation from unpurged duplicate content, which collapses once purging is applied. A second, fully independent team hit the same wall our own earlier draft hit, for the same underlying reason.

Correction (2026-09-02)

This entire document originally described a 30-candidate set that included MASY as one of "4 real missense substitutions" (§05, below). A subsequent funnel-gap audit (independent of NICEM, found while building an unrelated convergence comparison) determined MASY and 5 other candidates — including CLCN7 — are false positives: identical genotype in HS2 and ALE across all 3 independent HS2 baselines, most likely a reference-assembly base-call error, not real ALE-specific variants. The corrected set is 24 sure candidates, 3 confirmed missense (g8767 L82P, g1283 F207S, g5108 A350D — none of them MASY). Every count, table, and claim below has been updated to match. See the funnel-gap audit callout on the project summary for the full retraction record.

04

Candidate-level cross-validation

Using NICEM's independent assembly, all 24 confirmed candidates (not just the originally-named subset) were located by direct DNA sequence alignment (minimap2 asm5, mapq≥20, our HS2 reference against NICEM's raw HS assembly) — not by matching gene annotations, which differ between the two pipelines. NICEM's independent paftools.js variant calls were then checked within a 50bp window of each lifted-over position; a match with the allele as the reverse complement is still counted as confirmed, since it reflects NICEM's assembler representing that contig on the opposite strand, not a disagreement.

05

Why missense counts differ between the two analyses

Our own funnel-validated "sure candidates" (n=24, after the funnel-gap audit retracted 6 false positives including MASY) were individually classified for coding consequence against the AUGUSTUS gene model: 18 intronic, 3 intergenic, and 3 real missense substitutions — g8767 Leu82Pro (novel), g1283 Phe207Ser (fixed standing variant), g5108 Ala350Asp (fixed standing variant). MASY (Trp38Arg), reported in an earlier version of this document as a 4th missense candidate, is retracted: HS2 is homozygous for the "mutant" allele in all 3 independent baselines, identical to ALE — not a real ALE-specific variant. NICEM's own genome-wide single-copy substitution set, classified the same way, produced only one coding-consequence hit pair (a nonsense/missense call at two adjacent bases in the same gene) — the same adjacent-position clustering pattern already identified as a local-alignment artifact signature elsewhere in NICEM's data, not a credible independent finding.

One position tests clean, two are genuinely untestable this way — not the same thing

Checked directly: g8767 (novel/ale_only) has no variant call in NICEM's dataset at this exact single-copy-alignable position — a real absence worth taking seriously, most likely reflecting NICEM's lower assembly accuracy in this class of region rather than a problem with g8767 itself, but reported as an open point rather than explained away. g1283 and g5108 (both fixed standing variants) are a different, weaker kind of "absence": NICEM's raw assembly is a single unphased consensus of a heterozygous diploid genome, so a truly heterozygous site can be represented as either allele by chance — these two were not meaningfully tested by this comparison at all, independent of assembly quality.

07

Summary verdict & what remains open

What this establishes
  • All six original review points are resolved, and two (heterozygosity/duplication, assembly-size gap) are now independently reinforced by a second team's assembly hitting the same underlying issue.
  • 6 of the full, corrected 24-candidate point-mutation set, plus the 16kb duplication (quantitatively, +16,190bp of independent ALE-vs-HS length difference against our discovered 16,187bp), are confirmed by a fully independent assembly and variant-calling pipeline.
  • A systematic genome-wide re-screen of NICEM's own data found no additional credible candidates — the signal-quality tests (Ts/Tv, homopolymer fraction) point the same direction our own artifact-filtering already did.
Honestly still open
  • The 6 confirmed point mutations are all intronic — none of the 3 missense candidates (g8767, g1283, g5108) reproduced, for two different reasons (see §05): g8767 is a genuine, unexplained absence at a fair, single-copy test; g1283/g5108 were never meaningfully testable this way due to NICEM's unphased assembly at heterozygous sites. NICEM corroborates the methodology broadly, not the missense/structural-biology narrative specifically.
  • The ~1.5kb insertion is inconclusive (a genuine coverage gap in NICEM's data at that exact locus), not negative — it simply couldn't be tested.
  • Wet-lab Sanger confirmation of the named candidates has not yet been performed; primers are designed but unconfirmed. g8767 is now the highest-priority target given its NICEM non-reproduction — direct Sanger ground-truth is the most authoritative way to resolve that open question.
